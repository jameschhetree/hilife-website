Booking privacy

HiLife collects the name, email, phone number and session information you submit to arrange and manage studio bookings. Booking and payment-reference records are available to authorized studio staff according to their roles.

Team applications

The Join the Team form emails your application directly to hilife3nt@gmail.com through the studio’s connected Gmail account. The website stores a submission reference, a fingerprint of the application, and delivery status for duplicate protection and troubleshooting; application answers are retained in the business email, not in the website database. Submission records are kept for up to 90 days and removed during subsequent submissions. HiLife may use the contact details, experience and portfolio you share to review your interest and respond about opportunities. Please do not include sensitive identity documents or financial information.

Payments

Payments are completed through PayPal. This website stores payment references, amounts and status; it does not collect or store card numbers.

Studio integrations

If the owner connects Google, the application checks the selected calendar for busy times, creates or updates confirmed-session events, and sends booking alerts through the connected Gmail account to that same account. Google authorization tokens are encrypted on the server. These connections are for studio operations, not advertising.

Hosting and access

The application uses Vercel for hosting and Supabase for booking data. Staff sign-in uses an essential session cookie. A temporary checkout key is stored in your browser’s session storage to verify your PayPal return.

Questions or data requests

Contact hilife3nt@gmail.com or (470) 223-7797 to request access to or correction of your booking information. Payment records may need to be retained for operational or accounting requirements.
